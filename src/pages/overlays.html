Modal anatomy
Modals use data-bs-toggle="modal" / data-bs-target triggers and close via data-bs-dismiss="modal". Backdrop click and Esc both dismiss. Delete confirmations dispatch a confirmed event for page scripts.
Offcanvas anatomy
Offcanvas panels slide in from the right edge with a backdrop. Open with data-bs-toggle="offcanvas", dismiss with data-bs-dismiss="offcanvas".
Tooltip anatomy
Tooltips read data-bs-title on hover and position above the trigger. The title attribute is consumed so no native tooltip leaks through.
